Glimpses of India

CBSE Class 10 • English • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

A celebration of India’s cultural diversity and rich heritage, capturing the essence of Goan bakers, the brave people of Coorg, and the tea gardens of Assam.

2. Character Arc

The Pader (Baker): Traditional Goan baker, a symbol of Portuguese influence.
People of Coorg: Fiercely independent, hospitable, martial race.
Rajvir & Pranjol: Two youngsters exploring the tea estates of Assam.

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. What are the elders in Goa nostalgic about? [2]
Answer: They are nostalgic about the good old Portuguese days and their famous loaves of bread.
Q2. Describe the martial traditions of Coorg. IMPORTANT [3]
Answer: Coorgis are fierce warriors. The Coorg Regiment is one of the most decorated in the Indian Army, and General Cariappa, the first Chief of the Indian Army, was a Coorgi.
Q3. What are the two legends about the discovery of tea? [5]
Answer: One is the Chinese legend about an emperor whose boiling water was flavored by twigs falling into it. The other is an Indian legend about Bodhidharma, who cut off his eyelids because he felt sleepy during meditation, and ten tea plants grew out of them.

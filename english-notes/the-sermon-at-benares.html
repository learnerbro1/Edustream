The Sermon at Benares

CBSE Class 10 • English • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The chapter imparts a profound spiritual lesson: death is inevitable and universal. Grief and sorrow only increase pain; acceptance brings peace.

2. Character Arc

Gautama Buddha: The enlightened one who teaches the truth of life and death.
Kisa Gotami: A grieving mother who cannot accept the death of her only son until she realizes that death spares no one.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

InevitableMustard seedEnlightenmentValley of desolationAcceptance

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. Why did Kisa Gotami go from house to house? [2]
Answer: She went from house to house asking for medicine that could cure her dead son, refusing to accept that he had passed away.
Q2. What did Buddha ask Kisa Gotami to bring? IMPORTANT [3]
Answer: Buddha asked her to bring a handful of mustard seeds from a house where no one had ever lost a child, husband, parent, or friend.
Q3. How did Buddha make Kisa Gotami realize the reality of death? CBSE 2019 [5]
Answer: By sending her to fetch mustard seeds from a house that had never known death, Buddha made her realize that such a house does not exist. She understood that death is common to all, and that she was being selfish in her grief. Acceptance of death is the only way to attain peace of mind.

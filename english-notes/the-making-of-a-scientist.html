The Making of a Scientist

CBSE Class 10 • English — Footprints Without Feet • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The chapter emphasizes the importance of curiosity, a competitive spirit, and the right guidance in shaping a scientific mind.

2. Character Arc

Richard Ebright: A highly curious, competitive, and brilliant scientist.
His Mother: His driving force, who bought him equipment and a crucial book, "The Travels of Monarch X".

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

CuriosityButterfliesCompetitive spiritMother's supportDNA

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. What role did Ebright’s mother play in his success? [3]
Answer: Ebright’s mother encouraged his interest in learning. She took him on trips, bought him telescopes, microscopes, and cameras. Most importantly, she gifted him the book "The Travels of Monarch X," which opened the world of science to him.
Q2. What are the essential qualities of a scientist according to the author? IMPORTANT [5]
Answer: According to the author, the essential ingredients in making a scientist are a first-rate mind, driving curiosity, and the will to win for the right reasons. Ebright possessed all these qualities.

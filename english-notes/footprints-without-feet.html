Footprints without Feet

CBSE Class 10 • English — Footprints Without Feet • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The story explores the misuse of scientific discoveries. A brilliant scientist becomes a lawless person, showing that science without humanity is dangerous.

2. Character Arc

Griffin: A brilliant but eccentric and lawless scientist who discovers how to make the human body invisible. He uses his power for theft, revenge, and chaos.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

InvisibilityLawlessMisuse of scienceEccentricChaos

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. How did Griffin become invisible? [2]
Answer: Griffin swallowed certain rare drugs that made his body as transparent as a sheet of glass, though it also remained as solid as glass.
Q2. Why was Griffin wandering the streets of London? IMPORTANT [3]
Answer: Griffin had set fire to his landlord’s house in revenge. To escape without being seen, he had to remove all his clothes, becoming an invisible, homeless wanderer without money or clothes in the cold London winter.

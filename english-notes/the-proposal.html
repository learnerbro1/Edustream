The Proposal

CBSE Class 10 • English • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

A satirical play that mocks the materialistic nature of marriages in the upper-class Russian society, where economic security is prioritized over love and romance.

2. Character Arc

Lomov: A wealthy but hypochondriac landowner seeking marriage for security.
Natalya: A quarrelsome but excellent housekeeper who is desperate to get married.
Chubukov: Natalya’s father, who is eager to marry off his daughter to a wealthy neighbor.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

SatireHypochondriacOxen MeadowsGuess and SqueezerMaterialism

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. Why does Lomov visit Chubukov? [2]
Answer: Lomov visits Chubukov dressed in his formal evening wear to propose marriage to his daughter, Natalya.
Q2. What do Lomov and Natalya argue about first? [3]
Answer: They argue over the ownership of a piece of land called Oxen Meadows, both aggressively claiming that it belongs to their respective families.
Q3. How is the proposal finally made? IMPORTANT [5]
Answer: After multiple severe arguments and Lomov fainting from palpitations, Chubukov forces Lomov’s and Natalya’s hands together and gives his blessing. Even immediately after getting engaged, Lomov and Natalya start arguing again over whose dog is better.

Fire and Ice

CBSE Class 10 • English — First Flight (Poetry) • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The poem discusses the destructive power of human emotions. "Fire" represents desire, greed, and lust, while "Ice" represents hatred, coldness, and indifference.

2. Character Arc

The Poet: A philosophical observer predicting the end of the world based on human emotions. He equates human passions with destructive natural forces.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

Desire (Fire)Hatred (Ice)DestructionPerishHuman emotions

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. What do "fire" and "ice" stand for? IMPORTANT [2]
Answer: Fire stands for greed, lust, and unending desires. Ice stands for hatred, coldness, rigidity, and indifference.
Q2. To say that for destruction ice is also great—what does this mean? [3]
Answer: It means that silent hatred and indifference (ice) are just as capable of destroying human relationships and the world as fiery, passionate desires.

A Tiger in the Zoo

CBSE Class 10 • English — First Flight (Poetry) • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The theme is the cruelty of keeping wild animals in captivity. The poem contrasts the tiger’s majestic, natural life in the jungle with his helpless, suppressed existence in a cage.

2. Character Arc

The Tiger: Proud but helpless, angry but silent. He suppresses his wild instincts while pacing in a concrete cell, longing for his natural habitat.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

Velvet quietBrilliant eyesConcrete cellSnarlingCaptivity

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. Why is the tiger in "quiet rage"? [2]
Answer: The tiger is majestic and powerful but is locked in a small cage. He is angry at his captivity but remains quiet because he is helpless behind bars.
Q2. How does the tiger behave in the jungle? CBSE 2019 [3]
Answer: In the jungle, the tiger would hide in long grass near the water hole to hunt plump deer. He would snarl around houses at the jungle’s edge, terrorizing the villagers.

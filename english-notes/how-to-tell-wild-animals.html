How to Tell Wild Animals

CBSE Class 10 • English — First Flight (Poetry) • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

A humorous and slightly dark poem that suggests dangerous ways to identify wild animals. It relies on irony and dark humor.

2. Character Arc

The Poet (Carolyn Wells): Witty and sarcastic, providing fatal "tips" on identifying beasts based on how they attack or consume their prey.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

HumorIronyBengal TigerBear hugWeeping crocodile

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. How does the poet distinguish a hyena from a crocodile? IMPORTANT [2]
Answer: The poet says that a hyena comes with a merry smile, whereas a crocodile sheds tears while swallowing its prey.
Q2. What is the humor in the poem? [3]
Answer: The humor lies in the poet's suggestion that you can identify wild animals by allowing them to kill you. Calling a deadly bear’s attack a "hug" is a prime example of this dark humor.

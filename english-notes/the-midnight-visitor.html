The Midnight Visitor

CBSE Class 10 • English — Footprints Without Feet • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The chapter proves that presence of mind and intelligence are more effective weapons than guns or physical strength. Appearance can be highly deceptive.

2. Character Arc

Ausable: A fat, sloppy secret agent who uses his sharp mind to outwit his enemy.
Max: A rival spy who is armed but foolishly gullible.
Fowler: A romantic young writer who learns not to judge a book by its cover.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

Presence of mindSecret agentBalconyDeceptive appearanceOutwit

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. How is Ausable different from other secret agents? [2]
Answer: Unlike typical secret agents seen in movies, Ausable is very fat, sloppy, speaks with an accent, and lives in a gloomy French hotel.
Q2. How did Ausable outwit Max? IMPORTANT [5]
Answer: Ausable used his brilliant presence of mind. He fabricated a highly convincing story about a non-existent balcony outside the window. When a waiter knocked on the door, Ausable claimed it was the police. Panicking, Max jumped out of the window to hide on the "balcony" and fell to his death.
Success belongs to those who prepare for it!

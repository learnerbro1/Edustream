A Triumph of Surgery

CBSE Class 10 • English — Footprints Without Feet • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The theme highlights the dangers of over-pampering and blind love. It shows how excessive, irrational care can sometimes physically harm the ones we love, and how practical discipline, a proper diet, and exercise are often the best medicine.

2. Character Arc

Tricki (The Dog): Starts off as a hugely fat, lazy, and sick dog due to overfeeding. After spending time at the surgery, he transforms into an energetic, muscular, and happy animal.
Mrs. Pumphrey: A rich, foolishly indulgent mistress. She means well but her blind love nearly kills her pet. She remains naive till the end, believing Tricki underwent an actual "surgery."
Dr. James Herriot: A practical, observant, and wise veterinary surgeon who uses common sense instead of medicine to cure Tricki.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

Overfeeding Listless Convalescing Practical treatment Pampering

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. Why is Mrs. Pumphrey worried about Tricki? [2]
Answer: She is worried because Tricki became hugely fat, appearing like a "bloated sausage." He became completely listless, refused to eat his favorite foods, lay on the rug panting all day, and had bouts of vomiting.
Q2. Why was Tricki's recovery called "A Triumph of Surgery"? IMPORTANT [3]
Answer: Mrs. Pumphrey thought Tricki was suffering from a serious illness that required medical intervention. When Tricki returned to her completely healthy, active, and muscular, she assumed it was due to a highly successful medical operation. Thus, in her ignorance, she called his natural recovery a "triumph of surgery."
Q3. How does Dr. Herriot treat Tricki? Discuss his practical approach. CBSE 2018 [5]
Answer: Dr. Herriot used common sense rather than medicine. He realized Tricki's only fault was greed for food. He kept Tricki in his surgery, gave him no food for two days but plenty of water. Gradually, Tricki started showing interest in his surroundings. He had to compete with other dogs for food and ran around playing with them all day. This natural diet and vigorous exercise melted his fat and cured him completely without a single drop of medicine.
Success belongs to those who prepare for it!

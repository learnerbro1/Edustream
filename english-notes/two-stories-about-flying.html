Two Stories about Flying

CBSE Class 10 • English • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

Part 1 (His First Flight): Overcoming fear through courage and necessity.
Part 2 (The Black Aeroplane): Mystery, faith, and the will to survive against all odds.

2. Character Arc

Young Seagull: Timid, afraid to fly, but ultimately conquers fear when pushed by hunger.
Pilot (Narrator): Determined to reach home, takes a huge risk by flying into a storm, guided by an unknown force.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

CowardiceHungerLeap of faithStorm cloudsMysterious guide

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. Why was the young seagull afraid to fly? [2]
Answer: He felt that his wings would never support him and he would fall into the vast sea below.
Q2. How did the mother seagull trick the young seagull into flying? IMPORTANT [3]
Answer: She flew towards him with a piece of fish but halted just out of reach. Maddened by hunger, the young seagull dived at the fish, falling outwards and downwards, which forced his wings to open and he started flying.
Q3. Who do you think helped the narrator in "The Black Aeroplane"? [5]
Answer: It remains a mystery. It could have been the narrator’s own hallucination out of fear and desperation, or his own subconscious mind guiding him through the storm, as there was no other airplane registered on the radar.

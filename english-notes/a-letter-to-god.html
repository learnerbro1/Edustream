A Letter to God

CBSE Class 10 • English — First Flight (Prose) • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The story revolves around the theme of unquestioning faith in God and the irony of human nature. It shows how faith can give hope in the darkest times, but also highlights the lack of trust humans have in each other.

2. Character Arc

Lencho: A hardworking, naive farmer with immense faith in God. His arc remains steady in faith towards the divine, but he becomes cynical towards humanity.
Postmaster: A kind, compassionate, and empathetic man who decides to help Lencho to keep his faith intact. He represents the goodness of humanity.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

Hailstorm Unshaken Faith Irony Bunch of crooks Compassion

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. Why did Lencho say the raindrops were like "new coins"? [2]
Answer: Lencho’s crops were ready for harvest. The rain would bring a good yield and prosperity. Hence, he compared the large raindrops to ten-cent pieces and the little ones to fives, symbolizing money and wealth.
Q2. What is the irony in the story? IMPORTANT [3]
Answer: The irony is that Lencho blamed the post office employees for stealing his money, calling them a "bunch of crooks," when in reality, they were the ones who had collected the money out of charity to help him and keep his faith in God alive.
Q3. Describe the character of the postmaster. CBSE 2019 [5]
Answer: The postmaster is a fat, amiable fellow. He is highly empathetic and kind. When he saw Lencho’s letter addressed to God, he initially laughed but quickly became serious. He deeply admired Lencho’s unshaken faith and decided to help him by collecting money from his employees and friends. He gave a part of his salary too. He is a symbol of humanity, empathy, and selfless charity, expecting no reward in return.

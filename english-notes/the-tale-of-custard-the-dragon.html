The Tale of Custard the Dragon

CBSE Class 10 • English — First Flight (Poetry) • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

A humorous ballad that proves that true courage is shown in actions, not words. Those who boast may be cowards, while those who seem cowardly can be the bravest.

2. Character Arc

Belinda: A girl who boasts of her bravery.
Custard: A dragon considered a coward who begs for a safe cage, but proves to be the only brave one when a pirate attacks.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

BalladCowardiceTrue couragePirateSatire

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. How did the other pets tease Custard? [2]
Answer: They teased him by calling him Percival (a brave knight) mockingly, because Custard always cried for a nice safe cage.
Q2. How did Custard prove his bravery? IMPORTANT [5]
Answer: When a terrifying pirate armed with pistols climbed into the house, Belinda and all her "brave" pets fled in terror. However, Custard jumped up, snorted like an engine, clashed his tail, and swallowed the pirate whole, proving that he was the truly brave one.

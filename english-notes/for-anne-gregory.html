For Anne Gregory

CBSE Class 10 • English — First Flight (Poetry) • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The poem discusses the difference between superficial love (physical beauty) and spiritual love. It asserts that humans judge by appearance; only God loves us for who we are.

2. Character Arc

Anne Gregory: A beautiful young woman with yellow hair who wants to be loved for herself, not her hair.
The Poet: A wise speaker telling her the harsh reality of human attraction.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

Yellow hairSuperficial loveInner beautyDespairOnly God

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. What does Anne Gregory want to do with her hair? [2]
Answer: She wants to dye her hair brown, black, or carrot color so that young men will look past her physical beauty and love her for her inner self.
Q2. What is the central idea of the poem? IMPORTANT [3]
Answer: The central idea is that human beings are often captivated by external beauty (like yellow hair). Only God is capable of loving a person solely for their inner soul and character, irrespective of physical appearance.

Mijbil the Otter

CBSE Class 10 • English • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The theme is the unique bond between humans and pets, specifically focusing on the playful, intelligent, and mischievous nature of an unfamiliar pet like an otter.

2. Character Arc

Gavin Maxwell (Narrator): A loving pet owner who goes to great lengths to care for his unique pet.
Mijbil: An intelligent, playful, and mischievous otter who loves water.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

CamusfearnaHostile environmentCompulsive habitsMishapsPlayful

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. Why did Maxwell get an otter? [2]
Answer: After his dog died, Maxwell felt too sad to get another dog. He lived in Camusfearna, surrounded by water, which was a suitable environment for an otter.
Q2. What happened when Mijbil was taken to the bathroom? CBSE 2018 [3]
Answer: Mijbil went wild with joy in the water. He plunged and rolled in the bathtub, shooting up and down the length of the tub underwater, demonstrating his natural love for water.

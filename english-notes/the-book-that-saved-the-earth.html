The Book That Saved the Earth

CBSE Class 10 • English — Footprints Without Feet • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

A science-fiction play that highlights the importance of cultural context and interpretation, and mocks intellectual arrogance.

2. Character Arc

Think-Tank: The arrogant and foolish ruler of Mars who misinterprets Earth’s nursery rhymes.
Noodle: The smart apprentice who politely guides his foolish master.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

Mother GooseMisinterpretationIntellectual arroganceInvasionNursery rhymes

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. Which book saved the Earth from Martian invasion? [2]
Answer: An old book of nursery rhymes called "Mother Goose" saved the Earth.
Q2. How did Think-Tank interpret the rhyme "Humpty Dumpty"? [3]
Answer: When Think-Tank saw the picture of Humpty Dumpty, he thought it looked exactly like his own large head. He misinterpreted the rhyme to mean that Earthlings had discovered how to defeat him and were planning an invasion of Mars, which terrified him into fleeing.

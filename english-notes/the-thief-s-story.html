The Thief's Story

CBSE Class 10 • English — Footprints Without Feet • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The story emphasizes that human values, trust, and education can transform even the most hardened criminal. Kindness is more powerful than punishment.

2. Character Arc

Hari Singh: A 15-year-old experienced thief who betrays trust but eventually realizes the value of education and respect.
Anil: An easy-going, kind, and trusting writer whose immense goodness transforms Hari.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

TrustTransformationEducationConscienceForgiveness

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. Why did Hari Singh decide to come back to Anil? IMPORTANT [3]
Answer: Hari Singh realized that if he ran away, he would only remain a small-time thief. Anil was teaching him how to write whole sentences, which could make him a big, respected man. Anil’s immense trust and Hari’s own conscience brought him back.
Q2. Anil didn't hand Hari over to the police. Why? CBSE 2018 [5]
Answer: Anil was a compassionate man. He knew Hari had stolen the money but also realized Hari had repented and returned it. By forgiving Hari and paying him, Anil gave him a second chance, knowing that handing him to the police would ruin his life.

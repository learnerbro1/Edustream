Fog

CBSE Class 10 • English — First Flight (Poetry) • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

A short, beautiful poem emphasizing the silent, mysterious, and transient nature of the fog, comparing it to a living creature.

2. Character Arc

The Fog: Personified as a small, silent cat that arrives unannounced, observes the city, and leaves just as quietly.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

Cat feetSilentTransientHarborHaunches

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. How does Carl Sandburg describe the fog? [2]
Answer: He describes the fog as a cat that comes silently on little cat feet, sits looking over the harbor and city on silent haunches, and then moves on.

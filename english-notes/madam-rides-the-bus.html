Madam Rides the Bus

CBSE Class 10 • English • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The story explores a child’s innocent curiosity and her first encounter with the mysteries of life and death, marking a transition from childhood to maturity.

2. Character Arc

Valli: An 8-year-old girl who is curious, confident, careful, and independent. She meticulously plans her first bus ride without her mother’s knowledge.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

CuriosityMeticulous planningIndependenceMystery of death

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. What was Valli’s favorite pastime? [2]
Answer: Her favorite pastime was standing in the front doorway of her house, watching what was happening in the street outside, especially the bus that traveled between her village and the nearest town.
Q2. How did Valli save up money for her first journey? IMPORTANT [3]
Answer: Valli meticulously saved every stray coin that came her way, resisting every temptation to buy peppermints, toys, or ride the merry-go-round at the village fair.
Q3. What dampened Valli’s enthusiasm during the return journey? [5]
Answer: On her return journey, Valli saw the same young cow that had been running joyously in front of the bus earlier, now lying dead by the roadside, struck by a fast-moving vehicle. This sudden exposure to the mystery of death shocked her and dampened her enthusiasm.

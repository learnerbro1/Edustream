The Trees

CBSE Class 10 • English — First Flight (Poetry) • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

An extended metaphor for women's liberation or nature rebelling against human confinement. It depicts trees breaking out of a house to return to the forest.

2. Character Arc

The Trees: Symbolize confined nature or oppressed women breaking free from domestic boundaries to reclaim their rightful place in the world.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

ConfinementLiberationBreaking barriersForestRebellion

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. Where are the trees at the beginning of the poem? [2]
Answer: The trees are inside the poet’s house, their roots and branches struggling to break through the floor and windows.
Q2. What happens when the trees move out? CBSE 2018 [3]
Answer: As the trees march into the forest, the glass breaks, the winds rush to meet them, and the moon appears broken like a mirror caught in the branches of the tall oak tree.

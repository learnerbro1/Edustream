Amanda!

CBSE Class 10 • English — First Flight (Poetry) • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The poem highlights the struggle of a teenager against constant parental nagging and the desire for freedom, escapism, and solitude.

2. Character Arc

Amanda: A teenage girl who escapes into a world of imagination (as a mermaid, orphan, or Rapunzel) to avoid her mother's nagging.
Mother: Overbearing and critical, constantly instructing Amanda on her posture and habits.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

NaggingEscapismMermaidRapunzelSolitude

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. Why does Amanda wish to be an orphan? IMPORTANT [2]
Answer: Amanda wishes to be an orphan because she is tired of her mother’s constant instructions. As an orphan, she imagines she could roam the streets freely in peaceful silence without anyone scolding her.
Q2. How does Amanda use her imagination as an escape? [3]
Answer: She imagines herself as a mermaid drifting in a languid sea, an orphan roaming the streets, and Rapunzel living peacefully in a tower, all to mentally escape the harsh reality of her mother’s nagging.

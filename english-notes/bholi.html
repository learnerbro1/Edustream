Bholi

CBSE Class 10 • English — Footprints Without Feet • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The theme is the transformative power of education and compassion. It shows how love and proper schooling can turn a neglected, stammering girl into a confident and self-respecting woman.

2. Character Arc

Bholi (Sulekha): A neglected girl with pockmarks who stammers, but eventually becomes a bold, educated woman.
Teacher: A kind and encouraging woman who builds Bholi’s confidence.
Bishamber: A greedy, older man who demands dowry.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

NeglectStammerTransformative educationSelf-respectDowry

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. Why did Bholi’s parents accept Bishamber’s marriage proposal? [2]
Answer: They accepted it because Bishamber was wealthy, had a big shop, his own house, and was not asking for any dowry initially. They feared Bholi would otherwise remain unmarried due to her looks.
Q2. How did Bholi’s teacher play an important role in changing the course of her life? IMPORTANT [5]
Answer: Bholi’s teacher was the first person to speak to her affectionately. She encouraged Bholi, gave her books, and promised her she would speak clearly one day. Her love, patience, and education transformed Bholi from a frightened, stammering child into a confident, self-respecting woman who bravely rejected a greedy groom.

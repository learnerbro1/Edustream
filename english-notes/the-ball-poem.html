The Ball Poem

CBSE Class 10 • English — First Flight (Poetry) • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The poem teaches a philosophical lesson about loss and responsibility. Losing the ball symbolizes the loss of childhood innocence and learning to cope with the material losses of life.

2. Character Arc

The Boy: Young, innocent, and devastated by the loss of his ball. He learns the epistemology of loss.
The Poet: An observer who chooses not to interfere, knowing the boy must learn this lesson himself.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

Epistemology of lossPossessionsInnocenceGriefResponsibility

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. Why does the poet say, "I would not intrude on him"? [2]
Answer: The poet wants the boy to experience the grief of loss and learn to bear it independently. Offering him money or another ball would defeat the purpose of this life lesson.
Q2. What is the "epistemology of loss"? IMPORTANT [5]
Answer: The epistemology of loss refers to understanding the nature of loss and learning how to cope with it. The boy learns that in a world of possessions, things will be lost and cannot be replaced with mere money. It teaches him maturity and the harsh reality of life.

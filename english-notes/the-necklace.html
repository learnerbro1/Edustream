The Necklace

CBSE Class 10 • English — Footprints Without Feet • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Chapter Theme & Core Message

The story is a powerful moral lesson on the dangers of vanity, false pride, and discontentment. It teaches that one should be happy with what they have.

2. Character Arc

Matilda Loisel: A beautiful but deeply dissatisfied woman who dreams of a luxurious life.
Mr. Loisel: A simple, loving, and supportive husband.
Mme. Forestier: A wealthy friend who lends the necklace.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

VanityFalse prideDiscontentmentRuinFake necklace

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. Why was Matilda always unhappy? [2]
Answer: She was born into a family of clerks but felt she was destined for a life of luxury and wealth. Her modest living conditions constantly frustrated her.
Q2. How did the necklace ruin the Loisels’ lives? CBSE 2020 [5]
Answer: Matilda lost the borrowed diamond necklace. To replace it, her husband borrowed huge amounts of money at ruinous interest rates. They had to move to a cheaper room, and Matilda did heavy household chores for ten years to pay off the debt, losing her beauty and youth in the process. The tragic irony was that the original necklace was fake.

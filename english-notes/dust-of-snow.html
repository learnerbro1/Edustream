Dust of Snow

CBSE Class 10 • English — First Flight (Poetry) • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Poem Theme & Core Message

The poem conveys the theme that small, seemingly insignificant events in nature can have a profound and uplifting impact on one’s mood. It also challenges the negative symbolism of the crow and the poisonous hemlock tree, showing that nature in all its forms has the power to heal.

2. Character Arc (The Poet)

The Poet (Robert Frost): He starts off depressed, regretful, and sorrowful. However, a brief interaction with nature instantly transforms his state of mind to joy and gratitude, saving the rest of his day from being wasted in regret.

3. Important Keywords to Highlight in Exams

Hemlock tree Crow Change of mood Healing power of nature

4. PYQ & Practice Questions

Q1. What do the crow and hemlock tree represent? [2]
Answer: Usually, a crow and a poisonous hemlock tree represent sorrow, gloom, and inauspiciousness. However, the poet breaks this convention and uses them positively, as they are the agents that bring joy and uplift his depressed mood.
Q2. How does the poet’s mood change? IMPORTANT [3]
Answer: The poet was initially in a state of depression and despair, feeling that his day was ruined. When a crow shook the fine dust of snow from a hemlock tree onto him, the sudden coldness and silent beauty of nature instantly shifted his mood from sad to joyful, saving the rest of his day.
Q3. What is the underlying message of the poem 'Dust of Snow'? CBSE 2020 [5]
Answer: The underlying message is that the healing power of nature is immense. Sometimes, even the smallest, most trivial incidents can bring about a drastic positive change in our state of mind. Furthermore, the poet teaches us not to be bound by superstitions. Things traditionally associated with bad omens (like the crow and hemlock tree) can also bring happiness and joy. Nature does not discriminate.

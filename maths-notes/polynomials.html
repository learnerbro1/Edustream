Polynomials

CBSE Class 10 • Maths — Chapter 2 • Quick Revision Sheet + PYQs

1. Chapter Formulas

Relationship between zeroes and coefficients of a Quadratic Polynomial:
For a quadratic polynomial p(x) = ax² + bx + c with zeroes α and β:
  • Sum of zeroes (α + β) = -b / a = -(Coefficient of x) / (Coefficient of x²)
  • Product of zeroes (αβ) = c / a = (Constant term) / (Coefficient of x²)

Formation of a Quadratic Polynomial:
If α and β are the zeroes, the polynomial is given by:
k [ x² - (α + β)x + αβ ] (where k is a non-zero constant)

2. Important Concepts

  • Geometrical Meaning of Zeroes: The zeroes of a polynomial p(x) are precisely the x-coordinates of the points where the graph of y = p(x) intersects the x-axis. A polynomial of degree n can have at most n zeroes.
  • Types of Polynomials:
    • Linear Polynomial: Degree 1 (e.g., ax + b). Maximum 1 zero.
    • Quadratic Polynomial: Degree 2 (e.g., ax² + bx + c). Maximum 2 zeroes. Its graph is a parabola.
    • Cubic Polynomial: Degree 3 (e.g., ax³ + bx² + cx + d). Maximum 3 zeroes.

3. PYQ with Solutions

Very short / MCQ — 1 mark

Q1. Find the number of zeroes of p(x) if its graph intersects the x-axis at exactly 3 points. [1]
Solution: The number of zeroes is 3, because the zeroes are the x-coordinates of the points where the graph intersects the x-axis.
Q2. If one zero of the polynomial x² - 4x + 1 is 2 + √3, write the other zero. [1]
Solution: Sum of zeroes = -(-4)/1 = 4. Let the other zero be β.
(2 + √3) + β = 4 ⇒ β = 4 - 2 - √3 = 2 - √3.

Short answer — 2/3 marks

Q3. Find the zeroes of the quadratic polynomial x² + 7x + 10, and verify the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients. IMPORTANT [3]
Solution: Let p(x) = x² + 7x + 10
= x² + 5x + 2x + 10 = x(x + 5) + 2(x + 5) = (x + 2)(x + 5).
So, the zeroes are -2 and -5.
Verification:
Sum of zeroes = -2 + (-5) = -7. Also, -b/a = -7/1 = -7.
Product of zeroes = (-2) × (-5) = 10. Also, c/a = 10/1 = 10.
Hence verified.
Q4. Find a quadratic polynomial, the sum and product of whose zeroes are -3 and 2, respectively. CBSE 2019 [2]
Solution: Let the zeroes be α and β.
Given: α + β = -3 and αβ = 2.
The quadratic polynomial is given by k(x² - (α+β)x + αβ).
= k(x² - (-3)x + 2) = k(x² + 3x + 2).
Taking k = 1, the required polynomial is x² + 3x + 2.

Long answer / HOTS — 5 marks

Q5. If α and β are the zeroes of the quadratic polynomial f(x) = x² - p(x + 1) - c, show that (α + 1)(β + 1) = 1 - c. HOTS [5]
Solution: f(x) = x² - px - p - c = x² - px - (p + c).
Here, a = 1, b = -p, constant term = -(p + c).
Sum of zeroes: α + β = -(-p)/1 = p.
Product of zeroes: αβ = -(p + c)/1 = -p - c.
LHS = (α + 1)(β + 1) = αβ + α + β + 1
Substitute the values:
= (-p - c) + (p) + 1 = -p - c + p + 1 = 1 - c = RHS.
Hence proved.

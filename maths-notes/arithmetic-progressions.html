Arithmetic Progressions

CBSE Class 10 • Maths — Chapter 5 • Quick Revision Sheet + PYQs

1. Chapter Formulas

General form of an A.P.: a, a+d, a+2d, a+3d, ...

nth term of an A.P. (an):
an = a + (n - 1)d

Sum of first n terms (Sn):
Sn = n/2 [ 2a + (n - 1)d ]
Sn = n/2 [ a + l ] (where l is the last term)

2. Important Concepts

  • Common Difference (d): Difference between consecutive terms. d = a2 - a1 = a3 - a2. It can be positive, negative, or zero.
  • If a, b, c are in A.P., then 2b = a + c (i.e., b is the arithmetic mean of a and c).
  • Selection of terms in A.P.:
    • 3 terms: a-d, a, a+d
    • 4 terms: a-3d, a-d, a+d, a+3d
    • 5 terms: a-2d, a-d, a, a+d, a+2d

3. PYQ with Solutions

Very short / MCQ — 1 mark

Q1. Find the 10th term of the A.P. 2, 7, 12, ... [1]
Solution: Here a = 2, d = 7 - 2 = 5.
a10 = a + 9d = 2 + 9(5) = 2 + 45 = 47.

Short answer — 2/3 marks

Q2. Which term of the A.P. 3, 8, 13, 18, ... is 78? CBSE 2019 [2]
Solution: Here a = 3, d = 5. Let an = 78.
a + (n - 1)d = 78 ⇒ 3 + (n - 1)5 = 78
⇒ (n - 1)5 = 75 ⇒ n - 1 = 15 ⇒ n = 16.
It is the 16th term.
Q3. Find the sum of the first 22 terms of the A.P. 8, 3, -2, ... IMPORTANT [3]
Solution: Here a = 8, d = 3 - 8 = -5, n = 22.
Sn = n/2 [2a + (n - 1)d]
S22 = 22/2 [ 2(8) + (22 - 1)(-5) ] = 11 [ 16 + 21(-5) ]
= 11 [ 16 - 105 ] = 11 × (-89) = -979.

Long answer / HOTS — 5 marks

Q4. The sum of the 4th and 8th terms of an A.P. is 24, and the sum of the 6th and 10th terms is 44. Find the first three terms of the A.P. HOTS CBSE 2018 [5]
Solution: Given: a4 + a8 = 24 ⇒ (a + 3d) + (a + 7d) = 24 ⇒ 2a + 10d = 24 ⇒ a + 5d = 12 ... (i)
Also given: a6 + a10 = 44 ⇒ (a + 5d) + (a + 9d) = 44 ⇒ 2a + 14d = 44 ⇒ a + 7d = 22 ... (ii)
Subtracting (i) from (ii): (a + 7d) - (a + 5d) = 22 - 12 ⇒ 2d = 10 ⇒ d = 5.
Substitute d in (i): a + 5(5) = 12 ⇒ a + 25 = 12 ⇒ a = -13.
The first three terms are a, a+d, a+2d: -13, -8, -3.
Step by step progress leads to success!

Surface Areas & Volumes

CBSE Class 10 • Maths — Chapter 12 • Quick Revision Sheet + PYQs

1. Chapter Formulas

Cube: TSA = 6a², Volume = a³
Cuboid: TSA = 2(lb + bh + hl), Volume = l × b × h
Cylinder: CSA = 2πrh, TSA = 2πr(r + h), Volume = πr²h
Cone: CSA = πrl, TSA = πr(l + r), Volume = (1/3)πr²h, where l = √(r² + h²)
Sphere: TSA = 4πr², Volume = (4/3)πr³
Hemisphere: CSA = 2πr², TSA = 3πr², Volume = (2/3)πr³

2. Important Concepts

  • Combination of Solids: When two solids are joined together, the total surface area is the sum of the curved surface areas of the visible parts. The volume of the new solid is simply the sum of the volumes of the individual solids.
  • Melting & Recasting: When a solid is melted and converted into another solid, the Volume remains unchanged. (e.g., Volume of cylinder = Volume of sphere).

3. PYQ with Solutions

Very short / MCQ — 1 mark

Q1. Two cubes each of volume 64 cm³ are joined end to end. Find the length of the resulting cuboid. [1]
Solution: Volume of cube = a³ = 64 ⇒ a = 4 cm.
When joined end to end, length of cuboid l = 4 + 4 = 8 cm.

Short answer — 2/3 marks

Q2. A metallic sphere of radius 4.2 cm is melted and recast into the shape of a cylinder of radius 6 cm. Find the height of the cylinder. CBSE 2019 [2]
Solution: Volume of cylinder = Volume of sphere
π × r² × h = (4/3) × π × R³
π × (6)² × h = (4/3) × π × (4.2)³
36 × h = (4/3) × 4.2 × 4.2 × 4.2 = 4 × 1.4 × 4.2 × 4.2
h = (4 × 1.4 × 4.2 × 4.2) / 36 = (1.4 × 4.2 × 4.2) / 9 = 1.4 × 1.4 × 1.4 = 2.744 cm.
Q3. A vessel is in the form of a hollow hemisphere mounted by a hollow cylinder. The diameter of the hemisphere is 14 cm and the total height of the vessel is 13 cm. Find the inner surface area of the vessel. IMPORTANT [3]
Solution: Radius r = 14/2 = 7 cm. Height of hemisphere = r = 7 cm.
Height of cylinder, h = Total height - radius = 13 - 7 = 6 cm.
Inner surface area = CSA of cylinder + CSA of hemisphere
= 2πrh + 2πr² = 2πr(h + r)
= 2 × (22/7) × 7 × (6 + 7) = 44 × 13 = 572 cm².

Long answer / HOTS — 5 marks

Q4. A wooden article was made by scooping out a hemisphere from each end of a solid cylinder. If the height of the cylinder is 10 cm, and its base is of radius 3.5 cm, find the total surface area of the article. HOTS CBSE 2018 [5]
Solution: Radius r = 3.5 cm = 7/2 cm. Height h = 10 cm.
When hemispheres are scooped out, the new surface created is their curved surfaces.
Total Surface Area = CSA of cylinder + 2 × (CSA of hemisphere)
= 2πrh + 2(2πr²) = 2πr(h + 2r)
= 2 × (22/7) × (7/2) × [ 10 + 2(3.5) ]
= 22 × [ 10 + 7 ] = 22 × 17 = 374 cm².

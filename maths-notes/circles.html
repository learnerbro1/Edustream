Circles

CBSE Class 10 • Maths — Chapter 10 • Quick Revision Sheet + PYQs

1. Chapter Formulas & Theorems

Theorem 1 (Tangent at any point): The tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact.

Theorem 2 (Lengths of tangents): The lengths of tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal.

2. Important Concepts

  • Tangent to a circle: A line that intersects the circle at only one point. There is only one tangent at a point of the circle.
  • Point of Contact: The common point of the tangent and the circle.
  • Secant: A line that intersects the circle at two points.
  • Number of tangents from a point:
    • Inside the circle: 0 tangents.
    • On the circle: 1 tangent.
    • Outside the circle: 2 tangents.

Note: If two tangents are drawn to a circle from an external point, then they subtend equal angles at the centre, and they are equally inclined to the line segment joining the centre to that point.

3. PYQ with Solutions

Very short / MCQ — 1 mark

Q1. From a point Q, the length of the tangent to a circle is 24 cm and the distance of Q from the centre is 25 cm. Find the radius of the circle. [1]
Solution: Let the centre be O and point of contact be P. OP ⊥ PQ.
In right ΔOPQ, OP² + PQ² = OQ²
r² + 24² = 25² ⇒ r² + 576 = 625 ⇒ r² = 49 ⇒ r = 7 cm.

Short answer — 2/3 marks

Q2. Prove that the tangents drawn at the ends of a diameter of a circle are parallel. CBSE 2019 [2]
Solution: Let AB be a diameter of a circle with centre O. Let lines PQ and RS be tangents drawn at points A and B.
By theorem, OA ⊥ PQ and OB ⊥ RS.
So, ∠OAP = 90° and ∠OBS = 90°.
∠OAP = ∠OBS (Alternate interior angles are equal).
Since alternate interior angles are equal, lines PQ and RS are parallel.
Q3. A quadrilateral ABCD is drawn to circumscribe a circle. Prove that AB + CD = AD + BC. IMPORTANT [3]
Solution: Let the circle touch sides AB, BC, CD, DA at points P, Q, R, S respectively.
Lengths of tangents from an external point are equal.
AP = AS (from point A)
BP = BQ (from point B)
CR = CQ (from point C)
DR = DS (from point D)
LHS = AB + CD = (AP + BP) + (CR + DR)
= (AS + BQ) + (CQ + DS)
= (AS + DS) + (BQ + CQ) = AD + BC = RHS.

Long answer / HOTS — 5 marks

Q4. Prove that the lengths of tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal. IMPORTANT CBSE 2020 [5]
Solution: Given: A circle with centre O and a point P outside the circle. PQ and PR are two tangents from P to the circle.
To prove: PQ = PR.
Construction: Join OQ, OR, and OP.
Proof: We know that tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact. So, ∠OQP = ∠ORP = 90°.
In right triangles ΔOQP and ΔORP:
OQ = OR (Radii of the same circle)
OP = OP (Common hypotenuse)
Therefore, ΔOQP ≅ ΔORP (By RHS congruence criterion)
Hence, PQ = PR (By CPCT).
Everything comes full circle!

Probability

CBSE Class 10 • Maths — Chapter 14 • Quick Revision Sheet + PYQs

1. Chapter Formulas

Theoretical (Classical) Probability of an Event E:
P(E) = (Number of outcomes favorable to E) / (Total number of possible outcomes)

Complementary Events:
P(E) + P(not E) = 1 or P(&Ebar;) = 1 - P(E)

2. Important Concepts

  • The probability of a sure (or certain) event is 1.
  • The probability of an impossible event is 0.
  • For any event E, 0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1.
  • Playing Cards: A standard deck has 52 cards, divided into 4 suits of 13 cards each: Spades (♠, black), Clubs (♣, black), Hearts (♥, red), and Diamonds (♦, red).
    • Face cards: Jack (J), Queen (Q), King (K). Total face cards = 3 × 4 = 12.
    • Ace is usually not considered a face card.
  • Two Dice: When 2 dice are rolled, total possible outcomes = 6 × 6 = 36.

3. PYQ with Solutions

Very short / MCQ — 1 mark

Q1. If P(E) = 0.05, what is the probability of 'not E'? [1]
Solution: P(not E) = 1 - P(E) = 1 - 0.05 = 0.95.
Q2. What is the probability of an impossible event? [1]
Solution: 0.

Short answer — 2/3 marks

Q3. A card is drawn at random from a well-shuffled deck of 52 playing cards. Find the probability that the card drawn is a red face card. IMPORTANT CBSE 2019 [2]
Solution: Total number of possible outcomes = 52.
Number of face cards (J, Q, K) = 12.
Number of red face cards (Hearts and Diamonds) = 3 + 3 = 6.
Probability = 6 / 52 = 3 / 26.
Q4. Two dice are thrown at the same time. Find the probability of getting a sum of 8. [3]
Solution: Total possible outcomes = 36.
Outcomes with a sum of 8 are: (2,6), (3,5), (4,4), (5,3), (6,2).
Number of favorable outcomes = 5.
Probability = 5 / 36.

Long answer / HOTS — 5 marks

Q5. A box contains 90 discs which are numbered from 1 to 90. If one disc is drawn at random from the box, find the probability that it bears: (i) a two-digit number, (ii) a perfect square number, (iii) a number divisible by 5. HOTS CBSE 2018 [5]
Solution: Total number of discs = 90.
(i) Two-digit numbers are from 10 to 90. Favorable outcomes = 90 - 9 = 81.
Probability = 81 / 90 = 9 / 10.
(ii) Perfect square numbers from 1 to 90 are: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81. Favorable outcomes = 9.
Probability = 9 / 90 = 1 / 10.
(iii) Numbers divisible by 5 are: 5, 10, 15, ..., 90. This is an AP with a=5, d=5. Number of terms = 90/5 = 18. Favorable outcomes = 18.
Probability = 18 / 90 = 1 / 5.

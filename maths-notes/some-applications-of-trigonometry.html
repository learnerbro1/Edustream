Applications of Trigonometry

CBSE Class 10 • Maths — Chapter 9 • Quick Revision Sheet + PYQs

1. Important Concepts & Definitions

  • Line of sight: The line drawn from the eye of an observer to the point in the object viewed by the observer.
  • Angle of Elevation: The angle formed by the line of sight with the horizontal when the point being viewed is above the horizontal level (i.e., when you raise your head to look at the object).
  • Angle of Depression: The angle formed by the line of sight with the horizontal when the point being viewed is below the horizontal level (i.e., when you lower your head to look at the object).

Note: If the observer is at point A and the object is at point B, the angle of elevation of B from A is equal to the angle of depression of A from B (alternate interior angles).

2. How to Solve Problems

1. Draw a neat, labeled right-angled triangle diagram based on the question.
2. Identify the given side and the side to be calculated.
3. Choose the correct trigonometric ratio (sin, cos, or tan) that links the given side, the unknown side, and the given angle.
4. Most commonly used: tan θ = Perpendicular / Base.

3. PYQ with Solutions

Very short / MCQ — 1 mark

Q1. The shadow of a tower is √3 times its height. Find the angle of elevation of the sun. [1]
Solution: Let height = h, shadow = √3 h.
tan θ = P/B = h / (√3 h) = 1/√3.
Since tan 30° = 1/√3, θ = 30°.

Short answer — 2/3 marks

Q2. A ladder 15 m long just reaches the top of a vertical wall. If the ladder makes an angle of 60° with the wall, find the height of the wall. CBSE 2019 [2]
Solution: Note: The angle is with the WALL (vertical), so the angle with the ground is 90° - 60° = 30°.
Let height of wall be h. In right triangle, sin 30° = P/H = h / 15.
1/2 = h / 15 ⇒ h = 7.5 m.
Q3. A kite is flying at a height of 60 m above the ground. The string attached to the kite is temporarily tied to a point on the ground. The inclination of the string with the ground is 60°. Find the length of the string, assuming there is no slack in the string. IMPORTANT [3]
Solution: Let length of string be x. Height (P) = 60 m.
sin 60° = P / H ⇒ √3 / 2 = 60 / x
x = 120 / √3 = (120√3) / 3 = 40√3 m.

Long answer / HOTS — 5 marks

Q4. The angles of depression of the top and the bottom of an 8 m tall building from the top of a multi-storeyed building are 30° and 45° respectively. Find the height of the multi-storeyed building and the distance between the two buildings. HOTS CBSE 2018 [5]
Solution: Let the multi-storeyed building be AB of height H and the 8m building be CD. Let the distance AC be x. Draw DE ⊥ AB. So DE = AC = x, AE = H - 8.
In ΔABC (right angled at A), angle of depression of C is 45°. So, ∠ACB = 45°.
tan 45° = AB / AC ⇒ 1 = H / x ⇒ H = x ... (i)
In ΔBDE (right angled at E), angle of depression of D is 30°. So, ∠BDE = 30°.
tan 30° = BE / DE ⇒ 1/√3 = (H - 8) / x
Since x = H, 1/√3 = (H - 8) / H ⇒ H = √3(H - 8) ⇒ H = √3 H - 8√3
⇒ H(√3 - 1) = 8√3 ⇒ H = 8√3 / (√3 - 1)
Rationalizing: H = 8√3(√3 + 1) / (3 - 1) = 4(3 + √3) m.
The height is 4(3 + √3) m and the distance x = H = 4(3 + √3) m.

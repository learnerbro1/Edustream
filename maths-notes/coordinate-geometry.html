Coordinate Geometry

CBSE Class 10 • Maths — Chapter 7 • Quick Revision Sheet + PYQs

1. Chapter Formulas

Distance Formula: The distance between two points P(x₁, y₁) and Q(x₂, y₂) is:
d = √[ (x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)² ]

Section Formula: The coordinates of point P(x,y) which divides the line segment joining A(x₁, y₁) and B(x₂, y₂) internally in the ratio m₁ : m₂ are:
x = (m₁x₂ + m₂x₁) / (m₁ + m₂) , y = (m₁y₂ + m₂y₁) / (m₁ + m₂)

Mid-point Formula:
x = (x₁ + x₂) / 2 , y = (y₁ + y₂) / 2

2. Important Concepts

  • The distance of a point P(x, y) from the origin O(0,0) is given by √(x² + y²).
  • Collinear Points: Three points A, B, C are collinear if the sum of the lengths of any two line segments among AB, BC, AC is equal to the length of the remaining line segment (e.g., AB + BC = AC).
  • To prove a figure is a:
    • Square: Show all 4 sides are equal AND diagonals are equal.
    • Rhombus: Show all 4 sides are equal.
    • Rectangle: Show opposite sides are equal AND diagonals are equal.
    • Parallelogram: Show opposite sides are equal (or diagonals bisect each other using mid-point formula).

3. PYQ with Solutions

Very short / MCQ — 1 mark

Q1. Find the distance of the point (-3, 4) from the x-axis. [1]
Solution: The distance of a point (x, y) from the x-axis is its y-coordinate's absolute value. So, distance = |4| = 4 units.

Short answer — 2/3 marks

Q2. Find the point on the x-axis which is equidistant from (2, -5) and (-2, 9). IMPORTANT [3]
Solution: Let the point on the x-axis be P(x, 0). Let A(2, -5) and B(-2, 9).
PA = PB ⇒ PA² = PB²
(x - 2)² + (0 - (-5))² = (x - (-2))² + (0 - 9)²
x² - 4x + 4 + 25 = x² + 4x + 4 + 81
-4x + 29 = 4x + 85 ⇒ -8x = 56 ⇒ x = -7.
The point is (-7, 0).
Q3. In what ratio does the point (-4, 6) divide the line segment joining the points A(-6, 10) and B(3, -8)? CBSE 2018 [3]
Solution: Let the ratio be k : 1.
Using section formula for x-coordinate:
-4 = (k(3) + 1(-6)) / (k + 1)
-4(k + 1) = 3k - 6 ⇒ -4k - 4 = 3k - 6 ⇒ -7k = -2 ⇒ k = 2/7.
The ratio is 2:7.

Long answer / HOTS — 5 marks

Q4. If the points A(6, 1), B(8, 2), C(9, 4) and D(p, 3) are the vertices of a parallelogram, taken in order, find the value of p. HOTS [5]
Solution: The diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other.
So, the mid-point of diagonal AC is the same as the mid-point of diagonal BD.
Mid-point of AC = [ (6+9)/2 , (1+4)/2 ] = [ 15/2 , 5/2 ]
Mid-point of BD = [ (8+p)/2 , (2+3)/2 ] = [ (8+p)/2 , 5/2 ]
Equating the x-coordinates:
15/2 = (8+p)/2 ⇒ 15 = 8 + p ⇒ p = 7.

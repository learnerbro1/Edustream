Areas Related to Circles

CBSE Class 10 • Maths — Chapter 11 • Quick Revision Sheet + PYQs

1. Chapter Formulas

Circumference & Area of Circle: C = 2πr, Area = πr²

Length of an arc of a sector of angle θ:
l = (θ / 360°) × 2πr

Area of a Sector of angle θ:
Area = (θ / 360°) × πr²

Area of a Segment of a circle:
Area of Segment = Area of Sector - Area of the corresponding Triangle

2. Important Concepts

  • Sector: The region enclosed by two radii and the corresponding arc. (Think of it as a slice of pizza).
  • Segment: The region enclosed by a chord and the corresponding arc.
  • If angle θ = 180°, the sector becomes a semicircle. Area = (1/2)πr².
  • If angle θ = 90°, the sector is a quadrant. Area = (1/4)πr².
  • Clock minute hand: Sweeps an angle of 360° in 60 minutes. Therefore, angle swept in 1 minute = 6°.

3. PYQ with Solutions

Very short / MCQ — 1 mark

Q1. Find the area of a quadrant of a circle whose circumference is 22 cm. [1]
Solution: 2πr = 22 ⇒ 2 × (22/7) × r = 22 ⇒ r = 7/2 cm.
Area of quadrant = (1/4)πr² = (1/4) × (22/7) × (7/2) × (7/2) = 77/8 cm².

Short answer — 2/3 marks

Q2. The length of the minute hand of a clock is 14 cm. Find the area swept by the minute hand in 5 minutes. CBSE 2019 [2]
Solution: Angle swept in 60 min = 360°.
Angle swept in 5 min, θ = (360/60) × 5 = 30°.
r = 14 cm. Area = (θ/360) × πr² = (30/360) × (22/7) × 14 × 14
= (1/12) × 22 × 2 × 14 = (1/6) × 22 × 14 = 154/3 cm².
Q3. A chord of a circle of radius 10 cm subtends a right angle at the centre. Find the area of the corresponding minor segment. (Use π = 3.14) IMPORTANT [3]
Solution: r = 10 cm, θ = 90°.
Area of sector = (90/360) × 3.14 × 10 × 10 = (1/4) × 314 = 78.5 cm².
Area of right Δ = (1/2) × base × height = (1/2) × 10 × 10 = 50 cm².
Area of minor segment = Area of sector - Area of Δ = 78.5 - 50 = 28.5 cm².

Long answer / HOTS — 5 marks

Q4. Find the area of the shaded region if ABCD is a square of side 14 cm and APD and BPC are semicircles. HOTS CBSE 2018 [5]
Solution: Area of square ABCD = side × side = 14 × 14 = 196 cm².
Diameter of each semicircle = side of square = 14 cm.
Radius of each semicircle, r = 7 cm.
Area of two semicircles = Area of one full circle = πr² = (22/7) × 7 × 7 = 154 cm².
Area of shaded region = Area of square - Area of two semicircles = 196 - 154 = 42 cm².
Don't go round in circles, find the area!

Outcomes of Democracy

CBSE Class 10 • Political Science — Chapter 5 • Quick Revision Sheet + PYQs

1. How do we assess Democracy's Outcomes?

Democracy is considered a better form of government compared to dictatorships or other alternatives because it:

  • Promotes equality among citizens.
  • Enhances the dignity of the individual.
  • Improves the quality of decision-making.
  • Provides a method to resolve conflicts.
  • Allows room to correct mistakes.

Democracy is just a form of government: It can only create conditions for achieving something. The citizens have to take advantage of those conditions to achieve those goals.

2. Political Outcomes

Accountable, Responsive, and Legitimate Government:
  • Accountable: People have the right to choose their rulers and have control over the rulers. Regular, free and fair elections are held.
  • Responsive: The government is attentive to the needs and demands of the people and is largely free of corruption.
  • Legitimate: It may be slow, less efficient, or not always very responsive, but a democratic government is people's own government. That is why there is an overwhelming support for the idea of democracy globally.

3. Economic Outcomes

Economic Growth and Development: Evidence shows that in practice many democracies did not fulfill this expectation. Dictatorships have slightly higher rates of economic growth. However, economic development depends on several factors: country's population size, global situation, cooperation from other countries, economic priorities adopted by the country, etc.

Reduction of Inequality and Poverty: Democracies are based on political equality. However, growing economic inequalities are observed. A small number of ultra-rich enjoy a highly disproportionate share of wealth and incomes.

4. Social Outcomes

  • Accommodation of social diversity: Democracies usually develop a procedure to conduct their competition. This reduces the possibility of tensions becoming explosive or violent. Majority needs to work with the minority so that governments function to represent the general view.
  • Dignity and freedom of the citizens: Democracy stands much superior to any other form of government in promoting dignity and freedom of the individual. For example, the struggle for women's dignity and equal treatment, and the claims of disadvantaged/discriminated castes for equal status and equal opportunity.

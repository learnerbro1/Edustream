CBSE Class 10 • IT (402) — Part A Chapter 5 • Quick Revision Sheet

1. Sustainable Development

Development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

2. SDGs (Sustainable Development Goals)

17 goals adopted by all UN Member States in 2015 to end poverty, protect the planet, and ensure peace and prosperity.

3. Role in Sustainable Development

- Reduce, Reuse, Recycle (3Rs).
- Plant more trees and conserve water.
- Use eco-friendly materials and renewable energy sources.

PYQ Practice

Short answer — 2 marks

Q1. Define Sustainable Development. IMPORTANT [2]
Solution: Sustainable development is the economic development that is conducted without depletion of natural resources, ensuring that future generations can also meet their needs.

How do Organisms Reproduce?

CBSE Class 10 • Biology — Chapter 7 • Quick Revision Sheet + PYQs

1. Importance of Reproduction & DNA Copying

Reproduction is essential for the survival of a species on earth. DNA copying (replication) is accompanied by the creation of an additional cellular apparatus. Errors in DNA copying lead to variations, which form the basis of evolution.

2. Asexual Reproduction

Single parent is involved. No gametes are formed.

  • Fission: Splitting of a cell into two or more cells. Binary Fission: Amoeba, Leishmania. Multiple Fission: Plasmodium.
  • Fragmentation: Breaking of body into pieces, each growing into a new organism. (Spirogyra).
  • Regeneration: Ability to grow back lost parts into a whole organism. (Hydra, Planaria).
  • Budding: An outgrowth (bud) detaches and grows into a new organism. (Hydra, Yeast).
  • Vegetative Propagation: New plants develop from roots, stems, or leaves. (Bryophyllum leaf buds, Potato tubers). Ensures genetic similarity and faster growth.
  • Spore Formation: Spores germinate under favorable conditions. (Rhizopus/Bread mould).

3. Sexual Reproduction in Plants

Flowers are the reproductive organs.
Male part: Stamen (Filament + Anther containing pollen grains).
Female part: Pistil/Carpel (Ovary + Style + Stigma).

Pollination: Transfer of pollen from anther to stigma (Self or Cross pollination).
Fertilization: Male germ cell fuses with female germ cell in the ovary. The ovary develops into a fruit, and the ovules develop into seeds.
Fig 7.2: Longitudinal section of a typical flower

4. Sexual Reproduction in Humans

Male Reproductive System: Testes (produce sperms and testosterone), Vas deferens, Urethra, Penis, Glands (Prostate, Seminal vesicle add fluid).
Female Reproductive System: Ovaries (produce eggs, estrogen), Fallopian tubes (site of fertilization), Uterus (site of embryo development), Vagina.

Menstruation: If fertilization does not occur, the thickened lining of the uterus breaks down and comes out as blood and mucus.

5. Reproductive Health

  • STDs: Bacterial (Gonorrhoea, Syphilis), Viral (Warts, HIV-AIDS).
  • Contraception: Methods to avoid pregnancy.
    1. Barrier (Condoms - also prevent STDs). 2. Chemical (Oral pills). 3. IUDs (Copper-T). 4. Surgical (Vasectomy in males, Tubectomy in females).

★ Quick memory tips

Placenta: A special disc-like tissue embedded in the uterine wall that provides a large surface area for glucose and oxygen to pass from mother to embryo.

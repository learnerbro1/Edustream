Life Processes

CBSE Class 10 • Biology — Chapter 5 • Quick Revision Sheet + PYQs

1. Nutrition

The process of taking in food and using it for growth, metabolism, and repair.

Autotrophic: Organisms make their own food using simple inorganic material (CO₂ and H₂O) in the presence of sunlight. (e.g., Green plants).
Heterotrophic: Organisms depend on other organisms for their food. Types: Saprophytic, Parasitic, Holozoic.

Photosynthesis

6CO₂ + 12H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ + 6H₂O (in presence of Sunlight and Chlorophyll)

Major events: (i) Absorption of light energy by chlorophyll. (ii) Conversion of light energy to chemical energy & splitting of water molecules. (iii) Reduction of CO₂ to carbohydrates.

2. Respiration

The process of breaking down food (glucose) to release energy (ATP).

  • Aerobic: Occurs in the presence of oxygen. (Cytoplasm → Mitochondria). High energy yield (38 ATP). End products: CO₂, H₂O.
  • Anaerobic: Occurs in the absence of oxygen. In yeast (fermentation): Ethanol + CO₂. In muscle cells (during heavy exercise): Lactic acid (causes cramps).

3. Transportation

In Human Beings: The heart pumps blood. Arteries carry oxygenated blood away from the heart (except Pulmonary Artery). Veins carry deoxygenated blood to the heart (except Pulmonary Vein). Capillaries exchange materials between blood and cells.

Fig 5.2: Schematic sectional view of the Human Heart (Double Circulation)

In Plants:
Xylem: Transports water and minerals from roots to leaves (unidirectional).
Phloem: Transports food from leaves to other parts (bidirectional). Process known as Translocation.

4. Excretion

The biological process of removing harmful metabolic wastes from the body.

  • In Humans: Kidneys filter blood to produce urine. The basic filtration unit is the Nephron. Urine passes through ureters, stored in the urinary bladder, and exits via the urethra.
  • In Plants: Waste products are stored in leaves that fall off, as resins/gums in old xylem, or excreted into the soil. O₂ is a waste product of photosynthesis.
Fig 5.3: Structure of a Nephron (Filtering unit of kidney)

★ Quick memory tips

Stomata are tiny pores on leaves. Guard cells swell to open the pore (water flows in) and shrink to close it.

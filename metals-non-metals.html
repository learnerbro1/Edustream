Metals and Non-metals

CBSE Class 10 • Chemistry — Chapter 3 • Quick Revision Sheet + PYQs

1. Physical Properties

Metals: Solid (except Mercury), hard, malleable (can be beaten into sheets), ductile (drawn into wires), good conductors of heat/electricity, sonorous.
Best conductors: Silver (Ag) and Copper (Cu).
Non-metals: Solid, liquid or gas. Non-malleable, non-ductile, poor conductors (except Graphite), not sonorous.
Liquid non-metal: Bromine.

2. Chemical Properties of Metals

  • With Air (O₂): Metals form basic oxides.
    2Cu + O₂ → 2CuO (Black).
    Amphoteric oxides: React with both acids and bases to produce salt and water (e.g., Al₂O₃, ZnO).
  • With Water: Metal + Water → Metal Oxide + Hydrogen.
    Na and K react violently with cold water. Mg reacts with hot water. Fe, Zn, Al react only with steam.
  • With Acids: Metal + Dil. Acid → Salt + H₂ gas. (Note: HNO3 does not give H2 gas as it is a strong oxidizing agent).
Fig 3.2: Action of steam on a metal sample (e.g. Iron/Zinc)

3. Reactivity Series

A list of metals arranged in the order of their decreasing activities.

K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Pb > [H] > Cu > Hg > Ag > Au

4. Properties of Ionic Compounds

Formed by the transfer of electrons from a metal to a non-metal.

  • Solid and hard (due to strong electrostatic force).
  • High melting and boiling points.
  • Soluble in water, insoluble in solvents like kerosene/petrol.
  • Conduct electricity in molten state or solution (ions are free to move), but NOT in solid state.

5. Metallurgy & Corrosion

  • Roasting: Heating sulphide ores in the presence of excess air. (e.g., Zinc blende ZnS → ZnO)
  • Calcination: Heating carbonate ores in limited air. (e.g., Zincite ZnCO₃ → ZnO)
  • Alloys: Homogeneous mixture of two or more metals (or a metal and non-metal).
    Brass: Cu + Zn. Bronze: Cu + Sn. Solder: Pb + Sn (low melting point, used for welding electrical wires).

★ Quick memory tips

Aqua Regia: 3 parts Conc. HCl + 1 part Conc. HNO₃. It is a highly corrosive liquid that can dissolve gold and platinum!
